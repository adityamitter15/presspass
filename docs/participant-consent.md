# Participant information and consent

For people who agree to be recorded for the PressPass pushup dataset. Read this, ask anything you like, and sign or reply "I agree" only if you are happy with all of it.

**Adults only.** You must be 18 or over to take part. Nobody under 18 is recorded for this dataset.

## What you are asked to do

About three short sets of pushups in front of a phone placed on the floor, a few reps each. One set may be a deliberate "wrong" version (half reps, for example) so the counter can be tested against it. It takes about five minutes. Stop at any point if anything hurts or you would rather not continue.

## What is recorded

- The positions of 33 points on your body (shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips and so on) as numbers, about 30 times a second, while you do each set.
- The phone's own motion sensor readings during the set.
- How many valid pushups you did, the lighting, the distance from the phone and what you were wearing (for example "loose clothing").
- An anonymous label such as `S07` in place of your name.

## What is not recorded

- No video, no photographs and no audio are saved. The camera image is used on the phone to find the body points and is then discarded.
- No name, contact details, age, weight or other personal details are stored with the recording.

A list linking names to labels is kept separately by the project owner, only so that your recordings can be found and deleted if you ask. It is never published.

## How it is used

- To measure how accurately a pushup counter works on people it has not seen before, and to improve it.
- The body-point recordings will be **published openly on the internet** as part of a research dataset under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. That means anyone can download, use and share them, including for commercial purposes.
- Results may appear in a written report, a CV, and public posts. You will not be named.

A sequence of body points is far less identifying than a video, but it is a recording of how you move, and it cannot be guaranteed that nobody could ever link it to you.

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary. Saying no has no consequences of any kind.
- You can withdraw at any time. Before the dataset is published, your recordings will be deleted completely. After publication they will be removed from the published dataset and every later version, but copies that other people have already downloaded cannot be recalled.
- To withdraw or ask a question, contact the project owner: Aditya Mitter.
